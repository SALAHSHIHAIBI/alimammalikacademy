Privacy Policy & Terms

Al-Imam Malik Academy

What we collect

When you book a free trial or contact us, we ask for details such as your (or your child's) name, age, gender, country, WhatsApp number, email address, and your learning goals and availability. We only use this information to arrange your classes, match you with a suitable teacher, and communicate with you about your lessons.

How we use your information

  • To contact you on WhatsApp or email to arrange your free trial and ongoing classes.
  • To match you with a teacher of the same gender and a suitable schedule for your time zone.
  • To keep a record of your enrollment and lesson preferences.

We never sell or share your personal details with third parties for marketing purposes.

Cancellation policy

You may cancel your enrollment at any time, with no penalty and no long-term commitment. Simply let us know via WhatsApp or email, and we will stop scheduling further lessons from that point.

Free trial

Your first class with us is a free trial with no obligation to continue. You will only be asked for payment if you choose to proceed with a paid plan afterwards.

Contact us

If you have any questions about your data or would like to cancel, reach us via WhatsApp or by email.
